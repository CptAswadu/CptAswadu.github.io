About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Informatics at UTHealth Houston, advised by Dr. Kirk Roberts. My research focuses on developing reliable AI systems for clinical question answering.

Previously, I conducted research at Boston Children's Hospital and Columbia University, focusing on large language models for healthcare, rare disease diagnosis, and genetic testing workflows. I hold an M.A. in Statistics from Columbia University.
